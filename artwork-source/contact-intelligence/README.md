## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a completely new standalone artwork to replace the synthetic person in the reference contact-section screenshot. The screenshot is only a reference for placement and monochrome atmosphere, not an identity to preserve. Subject: an uploaded intelligence attempting to assemble a human-readable manifestation from information. Portrait 2:3 composition on a perfectly black background, no text, no interface, no grid or crosshairs. A luminous incomplete face and upper body coalesce from tens of thousands of extremely fine ivory points, delicate silver contour filaments, and broken horizontal slices of data. Face in a gentle three-quarter turn toward the viewer's left, contemplative, androgynous, ageless, calm and uncanny. The forehead, one eye socket, bridge of nose and mouth are just legible; the far side of the skull opens into flowing disconnected strands and sparse star-like points. No eyeball glow. An incomplete raised hand on the right reaches toward us with a subtle open, questioning gesture, individual fingers suggested by delicate bundles of fine threads. No hair, no clothing, no armor, no robot plating, no wires, no metallic solid skin. This should look like a consciousness negotiating a shape, not an android. The neck and torso unravel into a beautiful long downward current of fine filaments and individual points, fading completely before the bottom margin. Fine irregular gaps through the form; missing parts feel deliberately unresolved. Main face occupies the upper third, hand at middle right, wispy shoulders in the middle, drifting tails below. Very sparse tiny muted copper flecks near the hand and eye are the only color, less than 1% of the image. Exquisite restrained high-end generative-art installation, point-cloud portrait, airy luminous edges, deep rich black negative space. The whole figure and its particles fit inside the image with generous margins on all four sides. Avoid a solid photoreal human, comic-book character, dense mechanical detail, horror, skull, literal brain, mandala, halo, neon sci-fi glow, symbols, letters, labels, borders, and watermark. The background must remain pure black, not gray, for seamless screen compositing on a dark green website.
