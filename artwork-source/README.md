# Artwork sources

- `entity-07-signal-cartographer.png` is the original source generated for this
  portfolio with OpenAI ImageGen on 2026-07-27. It was prompted as an original
  non-human machine organism with no brand assets, known characters, or supplied
  third-party imagery. The shipped binary-alpha derivative is
  `public/assets/dither/entity-07-signal-cartographer.png`.
- `og-social-card-source.png` is the original cream/blue ImageGen social-card
  source from the first redesign pass.
- `og-social-card-archive-source.png` is the palette-only ImageGen revision for
  the mineral/petroleum/mulberry Archive theme. Its optimized 1200×630
  derivative is `public/og.png`.
- `og-social-card-framed-source.png` is the final ImageGen social-card pass. It
  preserves the portfolio typography while adding the inset research frame and
  a faint trace of the original signal cartographer. Its optimized 1200×630
  derivative is `public/og.png`.
- `project-illustrations/ares-frontier-engraving-source.png`,
  `edge-node-engraving-source.png`, `physics-engine-engraving-source.png`, and
  `inference-proxy-engraving-source.png` are original monochrome scientific
  plates generated for this portfolio with OpenAI ImageGen on 2026-07-27. No
  third-party artwork, Nous subjects, logos, interface screenshots, or known
  characters were supplied or copied. Their public derivatives are transparent
  4×4 Bayer masks under `public/assets/dither/`; the source plates stay here.
- `unverified/watcher-face-v2.webp` and `unverified/watcher-face-v3.webp` predate
  this redesign. Their provenance has not been confirmed, so they are preserved
  outside `public/` and are not referenced or shipped.

Project engravings, their technical underlays, ENTITY_07, and the
recursive-field poster are reproducibly generated by
`scripts/generate-dither-assets.mjs`.
